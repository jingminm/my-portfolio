Ann Arbor Go

Course project · team of 2 · Unity AR · my focus: landmark discovery, compass, squirrel encounters, and player avatar

Ann Arbor Go is an AR campus exploration game set around the University of Michigan. Players walk the real world in Exploration Mode, discover landmarks, plant trees, earn currency, and deal with Ann Arbor’s famously well-fed squirrels — then switch to Interaction Mode to battle them up close.

My work

On a two-person team, I built several of the core exploration and encounter systems:

  • Landmark discovery rewards — When a player taps a nearby landmark, a UI panel shows trivia, a photo of the real place, and a notice that a new seed type has been unlocked. A second tap closes the panel so they can keep exploring.
  • North-facing compass — A compass stays visible at the top of the screen and always points north, no matter how the player camera is oriented.
  • Squirrel encounters — Squirrels occasionally spawn near trees in Exploration Mode and cut currency output from nearby trees. In Interaction Mode, players can battle them while the squirrel uses evasive movement around the tree to dodge acorn throws.
  • Player avatar — A custom humanoid character that moves smoothly between locations and plays a walk-cycle animation while moving.

Gameplay systems

  • Exploration Mode & Interaction Mode
  • Camera orbit and rotations
  • Landmark information UI & seed unlock rewards
  • Seed shop & currency tracker
  • Real-time map / north-facing compass
  • Squirrel spawning, tree disruption, and battle encounters
  • AR image tracking
